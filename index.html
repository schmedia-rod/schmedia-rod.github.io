SmartRot

A private, single-user tool that uploads and schedules videos to the operator's own YouTube channels.

This application uses YouTube API Services.
By using it you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Information is handled according to the Google Privacy Policy.

What it does

  • Uploads finished short-form videos that the channel owner produced and reviewed to their own YouTube channels (YouTube Data API, videos.insert).
  • Sets title, description, tags and the scheduled publishing time.
  • Reads aggregated performance statistics of the owner's own channel (YouTube Analytics API) to improve future videos.

Who uses it

Only the channel operator. The tool runs locally on the operator's computer, is not publicly accessible and is not offered to third parties.

Data

No data of other YouTube users is collected. OAuth tokens and statistics are stored only on the operator's computer. Details: Privacy Policy · Terms of Service
